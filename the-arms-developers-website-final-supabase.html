// 08 SERVICES

Every parcel, one contractor.

Each service below stands on its own — or runs together as a single scope, from grading a raw plot to handing over a finished pool deck.

PARCEL A

Warehouse Construction

Pre-engineered and RCC-frame warehouses sized to your racking, load and dock layout — structural design through handover, built for industrial and logistics use.

PARCEL B

Land Filling & Grading

Cut-and-fill grading, controlled soil compaction and levelling that brings a raw plot to construction-ready grade and passes load-bearing checks.

PARCEL C

Land Survey

Boundary, topographic and layout surveys with total-station accuracy, so your title documents and the ground reality match before anyone breaks earth.

PARCEL D

Villa Construction

Custom villas built to your floor plan — foundation, structure, MEP, interiors and landscaping — managed under one site supervisor end to end.

PARCEL E

Swimming Pool Construction

RCC and shell pools with filtration, decking and tiling, engineered for residential plots or hospitality sites of any size.

PARCEL F

Society & Apartment Construction

Multi-unit RCC apartment blocks and housing society buildings — structural design, common areas and amenities, built through to OC-ready handover.

PARCEL G

House Construction

Independent and row houses built to your plan — foundation through finish — a lighter-weight scope than a full villa build, for standard family homes.

PARCEL H

Workshop Construction

Steel-frame and RCC workshops and fabrication sheds sized for machinery, power load and material movement — built for manufacturing and small-industrial use.

// HOW WE WORK

Five steps, one site file.

The same sequence runs whether we're grading a plot or finishing a villa — nothing hands off between separate contractors.

01

Site visit & brief

We walk the plot with you, note access, soil and your intended use before quoting anything.

02

Survey & soil testing

Boundary and topographic survey plus soil investigation set the baseline for design.

03

Design & approvals

Structural drawings, layout plans and the paperwork needed for local approvals.

04

Construction & supervision

A dedicated site engineer tracks progress, quality and safety through every stage.

05

Handover & documentation

As-built drawings, warranties and completion certificates handed over at close.
